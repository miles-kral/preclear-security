Why PreClear Exists

Trust should never be assumed.

We believe people and organizations should have a clearer way to understand what they are about to trust — before harm occurs.

The world changed

We live in an age of infinite digital trust.

Every day we are asked to trust things we have never met.

Emails Files QR codes Websites Messages Applications Identities Artificial intelligence

Most of our important decisions now happen through screens, systems, and networks.

Technology made information effortless. It also made deception easier to scale.

What we believe

The answer is not blind trust.

And it is not permanent suspicion.

It is verification.

The future belongs to people and organizations that can make trust decisions with more context, more evidence, and more confidence.

Our principles

Security should empower.

PreClear is being built around a set of principles that extend beyond any individual product or threat.

01

Confidence over fear

Security should help people make better decisions, not make them afraid to use technology.

02

Verification before trust

Trust should be earned through context, evidence, and appropriate controls.

03

Prevention before cleanup

Whenever practical, harm should be stopped before it becomes an incident.

04

Clarity over complexity

Security decisions should become easier to understand, explain, and act upon.

05

Evidence over assertion

Organizations should be able to show that preventative controls actually operated.

06

Responsibility over exploitation

Security should protect people when they are vulnerable, not take advantage of fear.

Security as a public good

A stronger ecosystem protects everyone.

We believe cybersecurity companies can create value beyond the boundaries of their own customer base.

Responsible disclosure

Support good-faith security research and responsible reporting of vulnerabilities.

Ethical intelligence sharing

Share useful, sanitized threat knowledge without exposing customers or sensitive data.

Security education

Help executives, small organizations, students, and everyday users understand how digital threats actually work.

Sustainable generosity

Use a disciplined portion of future capability to support organizations that create public value but lack resources.

Our mission

Protect people and organizations before damage begins.

PreClear exists to help make digital systems safer by moving verification and prevention earlier in the trust decision.
