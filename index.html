Pre-Ingress Security Infrastructure

Cybersecurity should begin before ingress.

PreClear Cybersecurity is building an upstream security layer designed to intercept threats before they reach people, systems, and data.

Modern security is exceptionally good at detecting compromise. We believe the next architectural shift is preventing more threats from entering the environment in the first place.

Why now

The perimeter did not disappear. It fragmented.

Cloud, SaaS, APIs, identity systems, shared files, and automated workflows created thousands of new points where trust decisions happen.

01

The old boundary

Security architecture was built around defined networks, managed endpoints, and centralized infrastructure.

02

The new reality

Business now operates across cloud services, SaaS applications, identity providers, APIs, remote users, and distributed data.

03

The architectural opportunity

Move inspection and enforcement upstream — closer to the point where external content and trust decisions enter the organization.

A new security layer

We are not trying to replace the security stack.

We are building the layer that comes before it.

Endpoint protection, SIEM, identity security, SOC teams, and incident response remain essential. PreClear's role is different: intercept more threats before those systems ever need to respond.

That architectural position is the foundation of our long-term strategy.

Know before you trust.

Every digital interaction asks us to make a trust decision. PreClear is being built to make that decision earlier, with more context and greater confidence.

The PreClear model
01
Encounter

A file, URL, identity, API request, or other external object approaches a trust boundary.

02
Verify

PreClear evaluates available signals before deeper access or interaction occurs.

03
Decide

The user, organization, or automated control receives a clearer basis for trust.

Technology has made information effortless. It has also made deception effortless.

The future belongs to those who know how to verify.
