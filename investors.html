Investor Overview

Building a new layer in cybersecurity.

PreClear Cybersecurity is building toward Pre-Ingress Security Infrastructure — an upstream control layer designed to intercept threats before they reach the systems, users, and data that traditional security must protect.

The investment thesis

The problem is not simply that threats are getting better.

The architecture of the modern enterprise changed. Security architecture has not fully caught up.

Cloud, SaaS, APIs, distributed identity, remote work, and automated workflows fragmented the perimeter into thousands of new trust boundaries.

Yet much of the security stack still begins after suspicious activity has already entered the environment.

PreClear is built around the belief that more security decisions should happen upstream.

Category creation

Pre-Ingress Security Infrastructure.

We are not trying to become another endpoint, SIEM, or scanning tool. The long-term goal is a horizontal control layer that operates before those systems become involved.

Business opportunity

Security first. Governance over time.

The immediate value is prevention and operational security. The long-term opportunity expands as prevention events become auditable control evidence.

Phase 3 Risk Quantification

Measure control effectiveness, exposure, and prevention impact over time.

Go-to-market

Start focused. Expand with the architecture.

The strategy is to use practical, high-value trust decisions as the wedge and expand toward broader enterprise control points over time.

Initial focus

Mid-market organizations

Security-conscious companies with limited staff and strong incentives to reduce operational risk.

Vertical expansion

Regulated industries

Finance, healthcare, SaaS, manufacturing, energy, and other environments where security and evidence both matter.

Long-term expansion

Enterprise & government

Larger environments where horizontal integration, governance, and risk infrastructure become increasingly valuable.
